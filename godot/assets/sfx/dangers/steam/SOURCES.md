# Sources: the Reactor's steam (DG6)

No files. Every sound of the steam is synthesised in code at run time by `godot/client/dangers/steam_view.gd`
(`_make_streams`), from a fixed seed, the same on every screen. Nothing recorded, downloaded or generated by a service.

- knock: a deep metallic knock in the line (a damped 110 Hz thump, a 1.4 kHz and 2.3 kHz ring, a hard click), 0.32 s.
  Two or three in each build, at the valve.
- build: the pressure building, 1.5 s: high-passed noise whose cutoff climbs from 2 to 5 kHz, rising 15 dB, a faint
  straining whistle.
- jet: the jet, a 1.85 s loop: broadband hiss with a flutter over low-passed rumble and a 55 Hz hum, crossfaded ends.
  Two emitters across the hall, one in the crawl.
- groan: the lines in a purge, 2.6 s: a deep bending tone (42 to 56 Hz) with a fifth above and rumble.
- cry: whoever walks into it, 0.62 s: a voiced pulse train (pitch jumping up then falling) through three vowel
  formants (750, 1220, 2650 Hz) with breath noise. A stand-in: a recorded take may replace it later under
  docs/VOICE.md's rules.
- ring: in your own ears while blind, 1.1 s: two close high tones (3150, 3185 Hz) fading.

Levels: each stream is scaled in code to its loudness in the game mix (godot/assets/sfx/MIX.md) and played through the
dangers base (Sfx.BASE_DB plus a trim that only lowers it); tests/test_danger_steam.gd measures each against its band.
