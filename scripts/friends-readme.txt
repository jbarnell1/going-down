GOING DOWN
Build of {{DATE}}, version {{COMMIT}}

Going Down is a short, spooky game for a group of friends. You ride a lift down together and find out what is waiting.

HOW TO START
1. Unzip this folder anywhere you like. Nothing needs installing.
2. Double-click GoingDown.exe.
3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". That is only because the game is not signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

HEADPHONES
Please wear headphones. The sound matters a lot.

PLAYING WITH FRIENDS
The game opens on a start screen. Choose "Join friends". It asks for three things:
- the server address
- your name
- the passphrase for tonight
The person who sent you this will give you the address and passphrase privately. Please do not post them anywhere public. Choose "Play alone" if you just want to look around.

CONTROLS
- W A S D or the arrow keys: walk
- Shift: run
- Ctrl (or C): creep quietly
- Mouse: look around (click the game first)
- E: bring out your instrument, and put it away again
- Right mouse button: hold the instrument up
- F: call the lift, hold the doors, pick up a tape
- T: switch the tape look on and off
- R: hold for the emote wheel, move the mouse to pick one, let go to play it
- Esc: free the mouse and show these controls

If something breaks, tell the person who sent you this.
