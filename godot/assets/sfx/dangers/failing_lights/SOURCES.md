# Sources: the failing ceiling strip

Every file here is synthesised from arithmetic by `assets_src/sfx/dangers/failing_lights.mjs` (sines, a seeded noise,
one-pole filters, written straight to 16-bit WAV; no tool or service). Rebuild with
`node assets_src/sfx/dangers/failing_lights.mjs`. Nothing was downloaded or generated by a service. Mono, 22.05 kHz.

Loudness is the base's simple measure (client/dangers/kind_view.gd `measure`: the RMS in dBFS of the samples above
-50 dBFS), each file in the middle of its category's band; the game plays every one at Sfx.BASE_DB through the base's
`play_cue` / `play_loop` (bus, unit size and range of the category). Sample peaks, measured: none over -6.4 dBFS.

- buzz.wav: the strip's ballast hum with a loose rattle and a few crackles, looped while it is lit (4.0 s; room, -26.1 dBFS, peak -18.2).
- wind.wav: the warning, the hum going rough, sagging and stuttering (2.0 s; effect, -22.1 dBFS, peak -10.6). Started part way in so it ends as the strip goes out.
- out.wav: the strip dropping out, the starter's tink, a dull thump, the hum ringing off (0.8 s; effect, -22.1 dBFS, peak -6.4).
- dark.wav: the starter trying while the strip is out, a tick and a faint fizz now and then, looped (2.0 s; ambience, -30.1 dBFS, peak -10.2).
- strike.wav: the strip catching, two ticks 0.35 s apart, the tube striking with a plink 0.35 s later, the hum swelling back (1.5 s; effect, -22.1 dBFS, peak -12.8).

The warning also plays the Clinic's own tube flicker (`clinic/flicker.ogg`, listed in the shared SOURCES.md) 3 dB under
the house level, so the local warning belongs to the same family as the lift's blink warning.
