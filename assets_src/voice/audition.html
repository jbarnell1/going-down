The elevator announcer: audition

Listen for a voice that sounds bright, warm and a little too slow on the normal lines, completely sincere on the bad news, and genuinely flat and cold on the slip; also listen for any tag read aloud as a word, odd sound effects, or a take that breaks character. Put your choice of voice, and any takes you prefer over take 1, on the hub's Questions page.

These are the clean recordings. In the game they play through a tinny elevator speaker that gets worse with depth. Nobody has listened to any of them yet.

Its own description is the default register: bright, warm, professional. Chosen as the default from the description alone (nobody has heard it).
